About Us

Over 20 Years of Excellence in Health Device Manufacturing

Leading Health & Wellness Device Manufacturer

Guangxi Maikang Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional health device manufacturer specializing in wellness solutions. Since our founding in 2005, we have grown into a leading supplier serving global markets.

With state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and an experienced R&D team, we deliver high-quality products that meet international health standards. We provide reliable and innovative wellness devices that improve the quality of life for users worldwide.

With a focus on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we have established long-term partnerships with distributors in over 80 countries.
