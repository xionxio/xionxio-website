02 STARTING QUESTIONS

The four questions that make a distributor enquiry usable.

Before asking for price or catalogue, define the market, buyer type, product reference and document categories your team needs to see.

MARKET

Where will the product be evaluated?

Country and channel change documentation, labelling and public-language requirements.

MODEL

Which record is under review?

Name ZY018G-I or ZY018G-I-2. Do not rely on a general product image.

FILES

What must be checked?

State whether you need a data sheet, IFU, labels, packaging, registrations or quality information.
